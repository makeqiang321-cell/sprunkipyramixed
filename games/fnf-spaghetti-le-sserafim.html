What Is FNF Spaghetti (LE SSERAFIM)?

FNF Spaghetti (LE SSERAFIM) is an official Friday Night Funkin' crossover with the K-pop group LE SSERAFIM, and it is not the same thing as the main game's Week 8. The mod takes the group's single "Spaghetti," featuring j-hope, and turns it into a fully playable rhythm track — the same arrow-note loop you already know, but set to a real LE SSERAFIM chart.

Boyfriend is the one on the mic, while Girlfriend shares the stage with Chaewon and the rest of the group shows up as animated characters. The stage pulls its look straight from the "Spaghetti" music video, so the backdrop and animations feel like a proper crossover rather than a reskin. It is a short, sharp track: hit the notes, keep your combo, and clear the song to see the tease that Week 8 is next.

How to Play FNF Spaghetti (LE SSERAFIM)

Controls

Hit notes with the Arrow Keys on PC. On mobile or tablet, tap the on-screen arrow buttons in time with the music.

FNF Spaghetti (LE SSERAFIM) Tips

Warm up in Freeplay

In FNF Spaghetti (LE SSERAFIM), Freeplay is the fastest way to learn the chart before the story run starts counting misses against you.

Read the note density

The chart shifts between simple and dense sections mid-song. Watch for the pattern change instead of holding one speed the whole way through.

Timing matters more than rushing. Press when each note is exactly on the target line, and the combo will build itself.
