What Is Sprunki Phase 19?

Sprunki Phase 19 is a creative fan-made music mod that expands the Sprunki universe with new characters, new sound loops and new animations. It keeps the familiar drag-and-drop music maker at its core, then pushes it in a playful direction: this phase is all about experimentation.

Each character is a specific type of sound — percussion, bass, melody, vocals or special effects. Bring performers onto the stage to trigger their loops, mix and match to build a track, and swap them at any time. The real fun comes from digging deeper: certain combinations trigger secret animations and bonus performances, rewarding players who try unexpected pairings. Whether you have been a Sprunki fan for years or just discovered the mod, Sprunki Phase 19 is an open, low-pressure way to get your musical creativity flowing.

Keep the percussion constant first, then layer melody and vocals on top. A steady beat keeps the whole mix in tune in Sprunki Phase 19.
