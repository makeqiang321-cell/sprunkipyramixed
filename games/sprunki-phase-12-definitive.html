What is Sprunki Phase 12 Definitive?

Sprunki Phase 12 Definitive is the finale. Where the other Sprunki phases build out the fan timeline piece by piece, this one closes it — a dark, industrial, "broken universe" chapter that ends the saga with the mixing loop you already know and a sound library that has gone fully to the dark side.

The setup is the same Incredibox formula: drag sound icons onto characters to layer beats, vocals, melodies and effects, and the loop locks to the tempo the moment each character lands. What makes the Definitive edition stand out is the flip. Add Black to the stage and the whole mix turns over — the bright opening gives way to distorted visuals and a horror-mode audio bed built from glitch, distorted bass, metallic clangs and eerie whispers. Around twenty characters fill the roster, and specific combinations unlock secret animations and lore for the players willing to experiment.

How to Play Sprunki Phase 12 Definitive

  1. 1

    Drag icons onto characters

    Assign a sound by dragging an icon onto a character. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Phase 12 Definitive.

  2. 2

    Tap to mute or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part from the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.
