What is Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters?

Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters is a remake of the Sprunki music mixer that swaps the familiar cast for the Sprunksters — a fan-made, Incredibox-style crew created by Bismeowth around six genre-based characters. "Retake" is the giveaway: this is a redo, not the original roster. The art, the cast and the sound library are rebuilt, while the loop you already know stays put.

Each Sprunkster covers a different role in the mix because each one is built around a different music genre — beat, bass, melody, vocals, effects, and so on. Drag them onto the stage and their loops slot together into one track. The whole thing is wrapped in a soft, cozy Club Penguin and MeepCity-style look, which makes this one of the friendlier, gentler corners of the Sprunki mod family.

Because the cast is organized by genre, Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters is easy to pick up — the characters almost mix themselves. You can still dig for hidden sounds by combining them in particular ways, so the depth is there when you want it. It lands as a chilled-out remix that's easy on newcomers and comfortable for veterans.

How to Play Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters

  1. 1

    Drag characters onto the stage

    Assign a sound by dragging a Sprunkster onto the stage. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters.

  2. 2

    Tap to mute or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part from the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Use it to build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.

  3. 3

    Combine for hidden sounds

    Certain combinations unlock hidden sounds. Pair specific characters or fill the stage in a particular order — Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters rewards curiosity.

Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters Tips

Start sparse, then stack

Begin with a beat and a melody, then add characters one at a time. A full stage can turn to noise fast if you rush in Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters.

Let the genres do the work

Each character covers a genre role, so a beat, a bass and a melody will already sit well together. Lean into that before you start experimenting.

Use headphones

The layered loops and hidden effects live across the stereo field. Phone speakers flatten them; headphones reveal what Sprunki Retake But Sprunksters is really doing.
