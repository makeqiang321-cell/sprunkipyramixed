What Is Sprunki Another One?

Sprunki Another One (also searched as Sprunki Vanilla) is a fan-made Sprunki mod in the Incredibox style. Built in Cocrea and playable free in your browser, it keeps the drag-and-drop loop mixing you already know and adds a brand-new face: Vanilla.

Vanilla is a Vanilla-White Sprunki who is shy but sweet. She dislikes the heat and prefers staying indoors, and her signature part is a soft ice-cream-truck tune. She joins Coco's circle of friends, giving the roster a gentle, pastel counterpoint to the louder characters. But beneath the sweetness there is a secret — Vanilla is occasionally controlled by a parasite, which surfaces in a hidden "corrupted" horror form.

Because the core gameplay does not change, Sprunki Another One is easy to jump into if you have played any Sprunki mod before. What changes is the flavour: Vanilla's warm, gentle loops sit side by side with a quietly creepy undercurrent that rewards players who dig deeper.

How to Play Sprunki Another One

  1. 1

    Drag characters to the stage

    Assign a sound by dragging a character onto the stage. The loop starts instantly and syncs to the tempo in Sprunki Another One.

  2. 2

    Click to mute or restart

    Click a character to pull its part out of the mix, then again to bring it back. Use it to build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.

  3. 3

    Combine for hidden sounds

    Certain combinations unlock hidden sounds. Experiment with pairings — Sprunki Another One rewards curiosity.

Sprunki Another One Tips

Start sparse, then layer

Begin with a beat and a melody, then add characters one at a time. A full stage of loops can turn to noise fast if you rush in Sprunki Another One.

Find Vanilla's ice-cream loop

Vanilla's ice-cream-truck chime is a soft top layer. Tuck it above a beat and a vocal to add a warm, gentle shimmer to your mix.

Hunt for the secret

Vanilla hides a parasite-driven corrupted form. Try unusual combinations and keep an eye on her — Sprunki Another One keeps its creepiness tucked away.
