What is Sprunki Swap 2?

Sprunki Swap 2 is the sequel to the Sprunki Swap mod, and its whole idea is in the name: every character has had their look, role and sound shuffled around. A face you recognize from classic Sprunki — Pinki, Wenda, Mr. Fun Computer — now carries a different loop, a different voice, or a different part of the mix. The roster feels familiar, but nothing sounds quite where you left it.

Underneath the swapped dressing, it is still the Incredibox-style mixer you know: drag characters onto the stage, layer their loops into a track, tap to mute or restart parts, and hunt for hidden combos. The twist is that placement and order matter more than usual — the same characters in a different sequence can change how the whole mix lands. With around 20 swapped characters, there is a lot of room to experiment.

Because so much of the cast has been moved around, Sprunki Swap 2 rewards people who already know the original roster — you get to rediscover each character's new sound — while still working fine for newcomers who just want to make a beat. It is a chaotic, experimental remix, and that is exactly the point.

How to Play Sprunki Swap 2

  1. 1

    Drag characters onto the stage

    Assign a sound by dragging a swapped character onto the stage. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Swap 2.

  2. 2

    Tap to mute or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part from the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Use it to build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.

  3. 3

    Rearrange for hidden combos

    Change the order and pairings to change the sound and unlock special animations — Sprunki Swap 2 rewards experimentation.

Sprunki Swap 2 Tips

Start sparse, then stack

Begin with a beat and a melody, then add characters one at a time. A full stage can turn to noise fast if you rush in Sprunki Swap 2.

Rediscover the roster

If you know the original cast, don't assume a face sounds like it used to. Test each swapped character before you build around it.

Play with order

The same characters in a different sequence can change the whole mix. Rearrange before you add more — Sprunki Swap 2 hides its best sounds in the ordering.
