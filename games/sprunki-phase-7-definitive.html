What is Sprunki Phase 7 Definitive?

Sprunki Phase 7 Definitive is Gabs' Take — a horror reimagining of Phase 7 by the modder GabsTheCreator. It keeps the drag-and-drop mixing loop you already know, but rebuilds the whole cast and sound library around body horror and a slow descent into musical madness.

The characters come back damaged and stitched together. Orange has a shattered face and glowing eyes, Gray is wrapped in bandages over an exposed skull, Raddy has an open chest cavity, Fun Bot is mid-melt, and Wenda is lankier and more psychopathic than before. The sound follows the visuals: darker, more distorted, and built to unsettle. Add the Black Key and the track drops into a lower, demonic key while the whole interface darkens — and combos like Jevin + Black unlock hidden lore and distorted messages for players willing to dig.

How to Play Sprunki Phase 7 Definitive

  1. 1

    Drag icons onto characters

    Assign a sound by dragging an icon onto a character. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Phase 7 Definitive.

  2. 2

    Tap to mute or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part from the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.

  3. 3

    Place the Black Key

    Drop the Black Key onto the stage to shift the whole mix into a lower, demonic key. It is the signature move of Sprunki Phase 7 Definitive.
