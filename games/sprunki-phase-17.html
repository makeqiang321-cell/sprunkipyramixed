What is Sprunki Phase 17?

Sprunki Phase 17 is a horror-themed fan phase in the Sprunki community. The drag-and-drop mixing loop you already know stays exactly the same — but the cast does not. Every character has been twisted, distorted and made almost unrecognizable, turning the familiar roster into what the community describes as a "flawed version of an old world."

Simon shows up carrying a saw. Clukr looks like a failed experiment. Mr. Fun Computer speaks in a voice built from SAM text-to-speech. Around them, the rest of the crew returns in unsettling forms: Fun Bot with his visor eyes, Mr. Sun, Raddy, OWAKCX, Garnold, Sky, Oren, Brud, Vineria, and Gray, who is still alive but harmed. It sits between Phase 16 and Phase 18, and it leans hard into unease rather than the brighter mood of the earlier chapters.

Because the gameplay is unchanged, Sprunki Phase 17 is easy to pick up for anyone who has played a Sprunki phase before. What changes is the feel: the distorted art and warped sound library make every mix come out darker than it would anywhere else.

How to Play Sprunki Phase 17

  1. 1

    Drag characters onto the stage

    Assign a sound by dragging a distorted character onto the stage. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Phase 17.

  2. 2

    Tap to mute or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part from the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Use it to build and break sections while the rest keeps playing.

  3. 3

    Combine for hidden sounds

    Certain combinations unlock hidden sounds. Experiment with pairings and arrangements — Sprunki Phase 17 rewards curiosity.

Sprunki Phase 17 Tips

Start sparse, then stack

Begin with a beat and a melody, then add characters one at a time. A full stage of distorted loops can turn to noise fast if you rush in Sprunki Phase 17.

Listen for the warped layers

The sound library is rebuilt around dread. Test each character before you build around it — the familiar face may carry an unfamiliar loop.

Use headphones

The distorted layers and hidden effects live across the stereo field. Phone speakers flatten them; headphones reveal what Sprunki Phase 17 is really doing.
