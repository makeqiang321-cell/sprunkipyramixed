What Is Sprunki Pyramixed Deluxe?

Sprunki Pyramixed Deluxe is the expanded deluxe edition of the pyramid-themed music mixer. It keeps the drag-and-drop core that made the original Sprunki Pyramixed a hit, then layers on everything the deluxe name promises: more characters and sounds, polished visuals, progression unlocks and optional lore scenes, all wrapped in an ancient Egyptian theme.

The idea stays simple and beginner-friendly. Drag sound icons onto characters to build a sound pyramid of beats, melodies, vocals and effects, then click a character to swap or remove its sound. What the deluxe edition adds is a sense of journey — as your mix builds, new characters, sounds and scenes open up, and the optional lore gives the Egyptian setting more depth for players who want to dig into it. Whether you are chasing a perfect track or just exploring the pyramid, Sprunki Pyramixed Deluxe meets you at your pace.

How to Play Sprunki Pyramixed Deluxe

  1. 1

    Drag sounds onto characters

    Each character is a sound type — beats, melodies, vocals or effects. Drag one onto a character to activate it in Sprunki Pyramixed Deluxe.

  2. 2

    Layer your sound pyramid

    Stack multiple characters to layer a full mix. Click a character to swap or remove its sound as you refine the track.

  3. 3

    Unlock as you build

    Keep building and experimenting to unlock new characters, sounds and lore scenes the deluxe edition hides up the pyramid.
