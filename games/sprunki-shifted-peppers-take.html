What Is Sprunki Shifted Pepper's Take?

Sprunki Shifted Pepper's Take is a horror-themed fan-made Incredibox mod created by the developer Pepper666. It takes the familiar drag-and-drop music mixer of Sprunki and drags it somewhere darker: the cast is reimagined as glitched, mutated, and distorted versions of themselves, rendered in bloody red tones and tied to the 12-Shifted storyline of betrayal, collapse, and infection.

The core loop stays simple and beginner-friendly. Drag and drop characters onto the stage to build a song, with each character contributing its own beat, bassline, or sound effect — up to 9 can perform at once. On top of the main roster, a hidden Alt Sounds menu adds 4 bonus effects to layer into your mix. The result is a music creator that feels genuinely unsettling, from its creepy visuals to its warped sound. If you like your mixes with a side of dread, Sprunki Shifted Pepper's Take delivers.

How to Play Sprunki Shifted Pepper's Take

  1. 1

    Drag characters onto the stage

    Each character carries its own beat, bassline, or sound effect. Drag one onto the stage to add it to the mix in Sprunki Shifted Pepper's Take.

  2. 2

    Stack up to 9 performers

    Keep adding characters to layer a full song. Click or drag a character away to change or remove its part while you refine the track.

  3. 3

    Open the Alt Sounds menu

    Find the Alt Sounds menu to unlock 4 bonus effects and layer them into your darker, denser mix.
