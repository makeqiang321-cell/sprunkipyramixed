What Is Sprunki Swapped Retextured?

Every character you know is in the wrong body. And every sound that body makes is completely different.

Sprunki Swapped Retextured takes the familiar Sprunki roster and scrambles it. Oren swapped with Raddy. Clukr traded with Garnold. Gray became Wenda. Ten character pairs, each one trading roles, sound libraries, and visual designs. A drummer becomes a vocalist and sings with percussive phrasing. On top of the swaps: a cute-to-horror toggle that flips every character between bright and corrupted versions in real time. Alternate sound cycling keeps your arrangements evolving on their own — characters auto-switch between sound variations as the mix plays.
