Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3 FAQ

What happens when the pyramid reddens?

The Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3 pyramid shifts from neutral to crimson when a character is placed in the wrong tier or when Durple destabilizes the mid-band without Tunner to counterbalance. Remove the offending placement or add the counterweight. Reddening reverses within seconds. Ignore it and the second stage — glitching — begins.

What does Sky actually diagnose?

In Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3, Sky reads frequency overlap and tier destabilization across the entire grid. It does not produce a musical loop — it produces a visual map of tension. Yellow signals friction. Red signals imminent punishment. Green signals equilibrium. Think of Sky as the pyramid's own nervous system, exposed and readable.

How does the greyed-out slot unlock system work?

In Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3, slots begin locked. Correct tier-matched placements unlock adjacent slots in sequence — base unlocks mid-band, mid-band unlocks apex. A wrong placement does not unlock anything. The pyramid withholds access until you respect its structure.

Why does Tunner counteract Durple?

In Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3, Durple's loop introduces phase cancellation and sub-frequency drift into the mid-band. Over time, this destabilizes the entire tier. Tunner emits a stabilizing harmonic that neutralizes the drift. Place both together and the mid-band reaches equilibrium. Place Durple alone and the pyramid punishes it.

What is the low hum warning?

Before the visual reddening begins, the Sprunki Pyramixed Phase 3 pyramid emits a sub-bass hum — audible only through headphones or quality speakers. It is the earliest warning signal. Catch it here and you can correct before any visual cue appears. Miss it and the reddening starts without preamble.
