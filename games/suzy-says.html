What Is Suzy Says Game?

Suzy Says Game is a short, unsettling Simon-Says-style horror game by the indie developer MOONBIT, framed as a mysterious interactive DVD. After an evening at a friend's house, you come home to find a cheerful character named Suzy smiling at you from the television, ready to play. The setup could not be friendlier. The catch is the one rule that runs the whole game: only do what Suzy tells you when she says "Suzy says." If the magic words are missing, you stay perfectly still.

The cheerful kids-show dressing is what makes Suzy Says stick with people. It starts like a long-lost DVD from 2003, complete with a too-bright smile and a too-enthusiastic host, then slowly curdles into something tense and wrong. You are not just remembering a sequence — you are deciding whether to obey a voice that is getting harder to trust, all while the room you are sitting in stops feeling safe. It is short, free, and built to be finished in one sitting.

Key Features of Suzy Says Game

One rule decides everything

The classic Simon-Says twist, but the stakes stop feeling playful almost immediately. Obey "Suzy says," and only that.

A cheerful show, a very wrong evening

Retro-DVD presentation and a bright, friendly host make the slow turn into horror land much harder on the nerves.

Unsettling turns, not cheap tricks

The tension comes from watching a face you were told to trust. Expect jumpscares and flashing lights along the way.

A quick one-sitting story

Short and free, built to be played through in a single evening with headphones on.

How to Play Suzy Says Game

Controls

Played with a keyboard and mouse on a computer. Headphones recommended — the audio cues matter.

  1. 1

    Watch Suzy

    She gives you commands from the television. Listen closely to exactly what she says in Suzy Says Game.

  2. 2

    Only obey "Suzy says"

    If the command starts with "Suzy says," do it. If the magic words are missing, stay perfectly still.

  3. 3

    Don't disappoint Suzy

    A mistake is not just a wrong answer. Keep the rule close and your eyes open until the evening ends.

Suzy Says Game Tips

Listen for the magic words

The difference between obeying and staying still is the two words "Suzy says." Train your ear to catch them every time in Suzy Says Game.

Use headphones

A lot of the tension and the cues come through sound. Headphones make the experience land the way it is meant to.

Mind the flashing lights

The game contains flashing lights and jumpscares. Take breaks or skip it if you are sensitive to flashing imagery.

Why Players Love Suzy Says Game

Suzy Says Game works because it takes a rule everyone already knows and quietly raises the stakes on it. You do not need to learn systems or solve puzzles — you just have to pay attention, and paying attention is exactly what makes it so uncomfortable when the cheerful host on the screen stops feeling cheerful. It is short enough to play in one sitting, free to start, and lingers far longer than it lasts. If you like horror that gets under your skin through mood and a single unforgettable face rather than gore, Suzy Says is worth your evening.
