What Is FNF Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight?

Friday Night Funkin' Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight is a fan-made rhythm game mod in the style of Friday Night Funkin'. It brings back Whitty — also known as Whitmore — a short-fused humanoid bomb-rockstar created by the modder Sock.clip, for an intense, fire-themed rap battle against Boyfriend.

The gameplay is the classic FNF formula: scrolling arrow notes rise up four lanes, and you press the matching arrow at just the right moment to keep the beat. Land enough notes and Boyfriend holds his own; miss too many and Whitty's fiery temper takes the song. The whole showdown plays out on a fire-themed stage that matches Whitty's explosive personality — and the mod's climactic track pushes the note speed and patterns high enough to test even seasoned rhythm players. If you have ever wanted a rhythm duel that actually heats up, this is it.

How to Play FNF Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight

Controls

Hit notes with the Arrow Keys or WASD. Enter starts and pauses a song, Esc pauses, and R restarts a run.

  1. 1

    Watch the arrows

    Arrows scroll up the four lanes toward the top of the screen. Keep an eye on the lane that is about to reach the target in FNF Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight.

  2. 2

    Press in time

    Press the matching arrow key or WASD key exactly when each note reaches the target to keep the beat and build your streak.

  3. 3

    Keep the streak alive

    Miss too many notes and Whitty's fire takes over. Hit consistently to survive the battle and chase a high score.

FNF Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight Tips

Lock into the beat

Rhythm games reward timing over speed. Listen to the music and press on the beat, not when you first see the arrow in FNF Whitty vs Boyfriend Fire Fight.

Use the lane above

Watch the upcoming lane as well as the current one so you are ready for fast note sequences before they hit the target.

Restart to learn the chart

Press R to restart and memorize tricky patterns. Repeating a hard section is the fastest way to push through the climactic track.
