What Is Sprunki Phase 32?

Sprunki has always been about dragging characters onto a stage and letting the loops do the work. Phase 32 turns the lights down and the neon up.

Sprunki Phase 32 is the cyberpunk neon chapter of the Sprunki music series. The core loop is unchanged — drop characters onto the stage to layer drums, bass, vocals and FX — but the presentation has gone electric. Neon visuals glow over eerie, layered soundscapes, and the glitchy UI bends and distorts along with the beats. It is the phase that feels most like a place, and least like a toy.

Where other phases reward rhythm, Sprunki Phase 32 rewards discovery. Certain character combinations unlock hidden scenes and bonus animations, and the game never tells you which ones. You build a track, the neon reacts, and the only way to see everything is to keep experimenting.

Sprunki Phase 32 Key Features

Cyberpunk neon visuals

A glowing, neon-soaked stage that reacts as you build. Sprunki Phase 32 looks like the sound feels — electric and slightly unreal.

Eerie, layered soundscapes

Loops stack into a dense, atmospheric wall of sound instead of clean separated parts. Layers overlap and drift into something bigger than the sum of their parts.

Glitchy UI that distorts beats

The interface glitches and warps as your mix grows, bending visually along with the rhythm. The frame becomes part of the music.

Hidden scenes and bonus animations

Specific character combinations unlock hidden scenes. Sprunki Phase 32 never hints at them, so experimentation is the only guide.

How to Play Sprunki Phase 32

  1. 1

    Drop characters onto the stage

    Assign drums, bass, vocals and FX by dragging a character onto the stage. Its loop starts instantly and locks to the tempo in Sprunki Phase 32.

  2. 2

    Tap to stop or restart

    Tap a character to drop its part out of the mix, then tap again to bring it back. Use this to build and break sections while the rest keeps running.

  3. 3

    Chase the hidden scenes

    Unusual pairings unlock hidden scenes and bonus animations. Nothing tells you which ones — Sprunki Phase 32 rewards trying everything.

Sprunki Phase 32 Tips

Start sparse, then layer

The soundscapes are dense. Begin with one drum and one pad, then add layers one at a time. In Sprunki Phase 32, a full stage can turn to mush if you rush.

Hunt the combinations

Hidden scenes come from specific pairings. Keep a mental note of what you have tried — the game will not log it for you.

Use headphones

The layered soundscapes live in the low end and the stereo extremes. Phone speakers flatten them; headphones reveal what Sprunki Phase 32 is actually doing.
