What Is Sprunki Kick the Buddy?

Sprunki Kick the Buddy is a chaotic sandbox crossover that drops the Sprunki cast into the stress-relief playground of Kick the Buddy. Forget the beat mixer — this one is pure cartoon mayhem. You unlock an escalating arsenal of tools, unleash them on a ragdoll target, and watch the over-the-top physics do the rest.

Simon is the star of the show as the target, but the familiar crew shows up to join in. Black, Sky, Tunner, Fun Bot and Wenda each appear as the action ramps up, and every character can transform as things get wilder. Knives, hammers, bombs and recovery kits all land with exaggerated, bouncy reactions. If you know Sprunki for the music, this is the opposite side of the coin — a lighthearted, no-pressure sandbox built purely to help you blow off steam.

How to Play Sprunki Kick the Buddy

  1. 1

    Pick your tool

    Choose from knives, hammers, bombs and recovery kits. Each tool behaves differently, so try them all in Sprunki Kick the Buddy.

  2. 2

    Unleash on the target

    Aim and fire at the ragdoll target, then watch the exaggerated cartoon physics send it bouncing around the sandbox.

  3. 3

    Watch the cast transform

    As the action escalates, Simon, Black, Sky, Tunner, Fun Bot and Wenda transform — keep going to see every wild form.

Sprunki Kick the Buddy Tips

Start with the basics

Knives and hammers are simple and satisfying. Get a feel for the physics before you reach for the bombs in Sprunki Kick the Buddy.

Save the recovery kits

Recovery kits reset the target so you can start a fresh chain. Use them when you want to re-roll the chaos instead of piling on.

Chain for spectacle

Combos and transformations are the real payoff. Mix tools back to back and watch how the cast reacts to a big finish.
