What Is Sprunki Pyramixed?

Sprunki Pyramixed is a fan-made Incredibox mod by developer Pyramix. It takes the drag-and-drop music format and drops it into ancient Egypt — pop-friendly loops are gone, replaced by tribal drums, techno arpeggios, and atmospheric chants. Characters wear pharaoh headdresses against pyramid backdrops. You drag them onto a stage and they play. Simple as that.

The sound is the thing. Kicks hit thick and distorted, rumbling through the low end like thunder across sand. Melodies spiral in minor-key arpeggios — somewhere between a Berlin techno club and a pharaoh's burial chamber. Characters pulse and flicker with the beat. The whole thing feels ancient, alive, and slightly dangerous. Free in any browser. No download. No sign-up.

How to Play

  1. Pick your characters： The bottom row shows every available performer — default roster characters plus any you have unlocked. Drag them onto the seven stage slots above. Each one loops a different sound: drums, synths, vocals, or effects. Swap them freely until the combination clicks.
  2. Build from the beat up： Start with a drum or percussion character to anchor the rhythm. Add one melody. Layer effects last — use them sparingly. The mute button is your best friend. Click a character icon to silence it, then bring it back two bars later for a drop.
  3. Trigger horror mode： Grab the black bowler hat from the corner of the screen and drop it on any character. Everything changes. The stage darkens, the music distorts, and the mood flips. Remove the hat to snap back. Try it on different character types — vocals become whispers, beats turn into industrial percussion.
  4. Record and share： When your track feels right, hit record. Perform your mix and save it to your browser. Make another one next week and compare them side by side — the difference will surprise you. Share your best recordings on Discord or Scratch.

Tips from Experienced Players

  • Start with three characters max： The most common beginner trap is filling all seven slots immediately — the community jokingly calls this Sprunki Pyramixed melophobia. One beat, one melody, one effect. Get those three locked in before adding anything else. A sparse track with space between layers sounds more professional than seven characters fighting for room.
  • Treat the mute button like an instrument： Cutting the drums for two bars and bringing them back creates a harder drop than any pre-made effect. Professional-sounding mixes use silence intentionally. Listen to your favorite tracks — they all do this.
  • Solo to find problems： Right-click or long-press any character to hear it alone. If two melodies sound muddy together, solo each one, identify which layer is causing the clash, and swap it out.
  • Keep horror mode to five or fewer characters： Too many active layers with the hat on can freeze the game. If it happens, just refresh the page.

How the Mod Came to Be

Developer Pyramix launched the mod on Scratch in early 2024. Two features set it apart from every other Incredibox variant at the time: a horror toggle triggered by a single accessory, and a text-code system that turned hidden characters into a community treasure hunt. The first code — pyramix — was discovered within days. Mard, Val, and Moch followed over the next month.

The Acid-plus-Tox combo that unlocked Sulfur was found by a player, not the developer — a moment that defined the community-driven identity of the mod. Phase 3 expanded the roster in mid-2024. Shifted brought an electronic remix later that year. Deluxe, Regretful, Swapped, Finished, 2013, and Pre Pyramixed arrived as community spin-offs. Over twenty variants now exist across Scratch, Discord, and itch.io.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created Sprunki Pyramixed?

Developer Pyramix built and published the mod on Scratch in early 2024. It is not affiliated with So Far So Good, the studio behind the original Incredibox.

How do I unlock the hidden characters?

Click the lock icon at the top of the screen. Type pyramix to unlock Acid and Tox. Mard, Val, and Moch each unlock one more. Place Acid and Tox together on stage to summon Sulfur. Codes sometimes vary between variants — check the community wiki for the latest.

What does the black bowler hat do?

It triggers horror mode. Place it on any character and the stage darkens, every loop shifts to minor-key, and character expressions turn unsettling. Remove the hat to return to normal instantly. The effect is different for each character type — vocals become whispers, beats turn industrial.

How do I get Mini Gray?

Mini Gray is one of several rare hidden characters. The exact unlock method varies between versions. Check the community wiki for current codes — what works in Phase 4 may not work in Shifted.

What does the ambience layer do?

Ambience adds background texture — drones, desert wind, subtle pads. It works best when running quietly beneath the other layers. Let it fill the space between your beats and melodies rather than pushing it to the front of the mix.

Can I play on my phone?

It works in mobile browsers, but drag-and-drop controls are designed for mouse or touchpad. Desktop gives the best experience. On a phone, rotating to landscape helps with precision when placing characters.

Is it free?

Yes. No payment, no account, no download. Open your browser, wait 30-45 seconds for the Egyptian assets to load, and start mixing.
