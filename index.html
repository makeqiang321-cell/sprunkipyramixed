    What Is Sprunki Pyramixed?

    Load Sprunki Pyramixed and the first thing you notice is the palette. Sand tones. Gold trim. Characters with pharaoh headdresses standing against a desert sky. This is not the bright, playful Incredibox you remember. Pyramix built this mod on Scratch in early 2024 as a complete reskin — every visual, every sound, every interaction reimagined through an ancient Egyptian lens. The beatboxers are gone. In their place: tribal drummers, synth priests, and vocalists whose chants echo off stone walls.

    The stage holds seven characters at a time. Drag one in and its loop starts immediately — no loading, no menus. Drums lock to the tempo. Melodies drift in a minor scale by default. Vocals hover above the mix with a raw, unprocessed texture that feels more ritual than performance. Then you spot the black bowler hat sitting in the corner of the screen. It doesn't belong. It's from another era entirely. That contrast is intentional. Drag it onto any character and Sprunki Pyramixed reveals what it actually is: the first horror music mod in the Incredibox community. Lights drop. Colors desaturate. Every loop on stage flips to a corrupted version recorded separately — not a filter, not a pitch shift. Whispers. Industrial percussion. Dissonance. Remove the hat and the light returns instantly. This single mechanic launched the entire Sprunki dark-mod ecosystem — from Phase 2 to Corruptbox.

    Sprunki Pyramixed Key Features

    Seven-slot live mixer

    Drop characters onto any of seven slots and their loops start instantly — synced to tempo, no loading. Swap characters mid-playback. Click any character to mute it for breakdowns. Sprunki Pyramixed runs entirely in your browser with zero latency between drag and audio.

    Black hat horror toggle

    Place the bowler hat on any character in Sprunki Pyramixed and every active loop flips to its separately recorded corrupted version — not a filter, not a pitch tweak. Stage darkens, colors wash out, drums turn industrial, vocals drop to whispers. Remove to revert. Stack hats on multiple characters to deepen the effect.

    Unlockable hidden characters

    Type pyramix into the lock icon to unlock Acid and Tox. Codes Mard, Val, and Moch each unlock one more. Place Acid and Tox on stage together and a third hidden character — Sulfur — appears. The game never tells you this. The community found it through experimentation.

    Built-in audio recorder

    Press the record button in Sprunki Pyramixed, perform your arrangement live, and the track saves directly to your browser. No external software needed. Record both clean and horror-mode versions of the same layout — they sound like completely different pieces.

    Egyptian sound library

    Every loop was built from scratch for Sprunki Pyramixed. Tribal frame drums replace beatbox kicks. Reed flutes and plucked strings carry melodies instead of synths. Vocal chants sit where pop vocals used to be. The palette is cohesive in Sprunki Pyramixed — every sound belongs to the same desert world.

    The original. Not a variant.

    Over 20 community mods trace their lineage here — Phase 3, Phase 4, Corruptbox, Shifted, Deluxe, Swapped. Every dark toggle, every unlock code, every pyramid-themed visual in the Sprunki ecosystem descends from Sprunki Pyramixed. This is the source. Explore all games.

    How to Play Sprunki Pyramixed

    1. 1

      Audition before you place

      Right-click any character to preview its loop solo. Every character in Sprunki Pyramixed has a distinct frequency range — the frame drummer sits in the low end, the flute melody occupies the mids, the chants hover above. Listen first. Placement is permanent until you swap.

    2. 2

      Build bottom-up

      Place the drummer first. Add a melodic character second. Vocals and effects go last. Three or four characters with space between them sound fuller than seven fighting for the same frequencies. The mute button is your best mixing tool — silence characters to create verses and drops.

    3. 3

      Deploy the black hat

      Once your clean mix is running, drag the bowler hat onto any character. The transformation happens in real time — use it on the chorus for impact, remove it on the verse to let the light back in. The horror toggle is a performance instrument, not a gimmick. Time it to the beat.

    4. 4

      Record both versions

      Press record and capture your full arrangement in standard mode. Then record it again with the black hat active. Same characters, same layout, completely different track. The Sprunki Pyramixed community on Discord and Scratch actively shares dual-version recordings.

    Sprunki Pyramixed Tips

    Enter codes before you start mixing

    Click the lock icon in Sprunki Pyramixed before placing any characters. Type pyramix first — it unlocks the most hidden characters. Then try Mard, Val, and Moch one at a time. Each code adds one character to your roster for the session.

    Place Acid and Tox together

    Unlock Acid and Tox separately using the pyramix code. Then place both on stage at the same time. After a few seconds, Sulfur appears in your character tray. The game never indicates this works — Sprunki Pyramixed players discovered it by accident.

    Tense and release with the hat

    Build a clean verse section. Drop the hat on the chorus. Remove it for the bridge. The contrast between clean and corrupted is what gives Sprunki Pyramixed its dramatic range. A track that stays in one mode loses the tension that makes this mod work.

    Headphones are not optional

    The frame drums in Sprunki Pyramixed have sub-bass resonance laptop speakers cannot reproduce. Stereo panning places each character in a distinct position. The corrupted vocal whispers sit in a frequency range phone speakers flatten into noise. Over-ear headphones only.

    Why Players Love Sprunki Pyramixed

    The appeal starts with the aesthetic. Most Incredibox mods remix the same bright pop palette. The seven-slot mixer is deep enough that two players with the same characters will produce completely different tracks based on placement order, muting patterns, and hat timing.

    But the real hook is the black hat. It turns music creation into performance. You are not just arranging loops — you are conducting a transformation in real time. The corrupted sound library was recorded separately, so the switch from clean to dark is genuinely unsettling in a way filters cannot replicate. Add the unlock code system — secrets the community is still finding years later — and you have a mod that rewards exploration long after the first track is done. Sprunki Pyramixed proved fan-made Incredibox projects could be original creative works. Every dark mod that followed — from Phase 3 to Pyramixed Phase 3 — is proof of that.
