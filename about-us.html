About Us

Welcome to sprunkipyramixed.app, home to Sprunki Pyramixed — the Egyptian-themed Incredibox mod created by Pyramix. We host free browser-based music creation games that you can play instantly with no downloads, no accounts, and no payments.

Sprunki Pyramixed is a fan-made mod that transplants the drag-and-drop music formula into an ancient Egyptian setting. Characters wear pharaoh headdresses against desert backdrops, and the sound palette blends tribal percussion with techno synths. The game features a unique horror mode triggered by the black bowler hat accessory, hidden characters unlocked via codes like pyramix, and over 20 community variants to explore.

We believe in keeping games free and accessible. Our platform works on school Chromebooks, restricted networks, and any device with a modern browser — no VPN needed, no admin permissions, no trace left behind.
